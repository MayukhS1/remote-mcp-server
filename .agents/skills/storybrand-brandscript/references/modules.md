# SB7 modules: questions, rules, examples

Interview one module at a time. Brainstorm 3–5 options, then decide on one.

## 1. Character

**Ask:** What is the one thing your customer wants that your brand helps them get? Which survival category does it hit (money, time, status, tribe, resources, generosity, meaning)?

**Rule:** One desire. Divisions and products get subplots later.

| Vague | Specific |
|---|---|
| Inhale knowledge, exhale success | Helping you become everyone's favorite leader |
| Competitive space program | A man on the moon |
| Excellence in landscaping | A yard that looks better than your neighbor's |

## 2. Problem

**Ask:** What villain causes the trouble? What is the external (tangible) problem? How does that make them feel inside? Why is that philosophically wrong (ought / shouldn't)?

**Villain checks:** root source · relatable · singular · real

| Brand | Villain | External | Internal | Philosophical |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tesla | Gas-guzzling, inferior tech | I need a car | I want to be an early adopter | My car ought to help save the environment |
| Nespresso | Machines that make bad coffee | Better coffee at home | I want to feel sophisticated | I shouldn't need to be a barista |
| Edward Jones | Firms that don't listen | Investment help | I'm confused | I deserve an advisor who explains things in person |

Customers buy solutions to **internal** problems. Companies often only sell the external one.

## 3. Guide

**Ask:** How do you say "we get it"? What 3 proofs show competence without stealing the hero role?

**Empathy openers:** "We understand how it feels to…" · "Nobody should have to…" · "Like you, we're frustrated by…"

**Authority (pick lightly):** ~3 testimonials · stats · awards · client logos

Brand-as-hero fails (Tidal: artists as heroes, customer question unanswered).

## 4. Plan

**Ask:** What 3–4 steps get them from hello to success? What fears would an agreement plan cancel?

Name the plan ("easy installation plan"). Process removes confusion; agreement removes fear.

## 5. CTA

**Ask:** What is the marry-me ask (direct)? What is the first-date ask (transitional)?

Direct: Buy / Schedule / Call / Register. Transitional: PDF, webinar, trial, sample. Always both.

## 6. Failure

**Ask:** What tragic ending are they trying to avoid? List ~3 (salt).

Fear appeal: vulnerability → should act → specific action → do it now. Too much fear gets blocked.

## 7. Success

**Ask:** What does life look like after? Be specific. Cover external, internal, and philosophical resolutions. Optional: status, wholeness, self-realization.

Before/after grid (have / feel / average day / status) helps brainstorm.

## 8. Identity

**Ask:** Who does the customer want to become? How do they want friends to describe them?

Write **To** first, then **From** as its opposite.

| From | To |
|---|---|
| Passive dog owner | Every dog's hero |
| Confused and ill-equipped | Competent and smart |
| Anxious and glum | Carefree and radiant |
